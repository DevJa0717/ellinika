# Road to B2: study plan, October 2026 to May 2027

Target: the B2 Certificate of Attainment in Greek (Πιστοποίηση Επάρκειας της Ελληνομάθειας), held once a year in May. Confirm the 2027 date and the registration deadline (usually late winter) on the Centre for the Greek Language site; this plan assumes mid-May.

Learner: an experienced polyglot. Grammar is already around B1; vocabulary is lower and grows more slowly than grammar; declensions are the weak spot. So the plan reviews A1-A2 grammar fast, puts the most daily time into vocabulary, and keeps extra time for case endings. Register: modern, everyday Greek at a high level, with ancient forms mentioned only as etymology. Start Monday 19 October 2026; 30 weeks.

Machine-readable copy for the app's tracker: `plan/b2-schedule.json` (week start dates, lesson ids, cumulative lesson and word targets).

## The shape of it

- **Weeks 1-3: grammar review, vocabulary sprint.** Test out of the 23 A1-A2 lessons (do the Practice set cold; open the Learn page only when you score below 85%). Week 3 is a declension week plus a placement mock to confirm B1. In week 1, also sweep the first 1,000 frequency words and mark the ones you already know; those count toward the targets.
- **Weeks 4-9: B1 grammar** (13 lessons) while vocabulary keeps the lead.
- **Weeks 10-16: B2 grammar** (15 lessons). Core vocabulary reaches 3,000 in week 14.
- **Weeks 15-21: B2 themes,** one a week, 1,000 thematic words. Week 17 is the second declension week; weeks 18-21 have no new grammar, only theme work.
- **Weeks 22-30: exam practice.** A full timed mock every week, then repair. This is also the buffer if vocabulary runs behind.

## A normal day (about 90 minutes)

| Block | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| Review | 20 min | Spaced-repetition review of words and forms due today. Never skip it. |
| New words | 30 min | About 30 new words a day until week 14, then about 20 a day of B2 theme words. |
| Declension drill | 10 min | Decline 3 of today's nouns with article and adjective through all cases, aloud (ο νέος φοιτητής, του νέου φοιτητή...). |
| Grammar | 10 min | The week's lessons: Practice until 85% or better. (Grammar is your fast skill, so it gets the least time.) |
| Skill | 20 min | Rotate: Mon/Thu reading, Tue/Fri listening, Wed writing, Sat speaking. |

Sunday is rest, or catch-up on words.

## Making vocabulary stick (the slow skill)

- **Learn nouns with article and genitive** (η κυβέρνηση, της κυβέρνησης), verbs with the aorist (γράφω, έγραψα), never bare words. This doubles as declension practice.
- **Learn in families.** Once the word-formation lessons are done (week 13), learn roots with their derivatives together: γράφω, γραφή, υπογραφή, συγγραφέας. From the start, note the ancient root when it explains a family.
- **Words in sentences.** Each new card shows its example sentence; say the sentence, not just the word.
- **Reading for coverage.** From week 4, read something every day at the edge of your vocabulary and add 5 words from it to the deck.
- **Watch retention, not just new words.** If more than 15% of due reviews fail for a week, halve new words the next week. The exam weeks absorb the delay.

## Checkpoints

| End of week | Check | If behind |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | All A1-A2 lessons at 85%+; B1 placement mock passed; 640 words known | Spend week 4 on the weak A2 lessons and shift B1 by a week |
| 9 | **Go/no-go:** 1,930 words known with under 15% review failures; all B1 lessons at 85%+; decline any noun from the first 1,000 in all cases without hesitating | More than two weeks behind on words: consider B1 in May 2027, or keep B2 and use the exam buffer |
| 16 | Every grammar and syntax item green on the tracker; first full mock | Use weeks 18-21 for weak grammar, not just themes |
| 21 | 4,000 words known; a second full mock at pass level on reading and listening | The exam phase becomes vocabulary-first |

## Extra time for declensions

- The daily 10-minute drill above, to the exam.
- **Week 3:** redo the Practice sets for gender, cases, masculine -ος, feminine/neuter, adjectives and the genitive to 95%+, then decline 20 lexicon nouns a day, one per declension class, with an adjective.
- **Week 17:** formal and learned patterns (κυβέρνηση/κυβερνήσεις, -ος feminines, -ον neuters, βαθύς/οξύς, ακριβής/συνήθης), then every class again under time pressure, reading genitive-heavy editorials.
- In writing corrections, log every case or agreement error; the most frequent ones become next week's drill nouns.

## Exam skills

- **Reading.** Weeks 1-9: simplified news and learner podcasts with transcripts. From week 10: opinion pieces and editorials; from week 15, on the week's theme. Note argument structure and linking words.
- **Listening.** Daily from week 1, as idle-time input; normal-speed radio and news from week 4.
- **Writing.** Weekly from week 1: 150 words (weeks 1-9), exam-length argumentative and formal texts from week 10, using the week's grammar and discourse markers (αρχικά, από την άλλη πλευρά, εντούτοις, συμπερασματικά). Paste each one to Claude in a thread for correction.
- **Speaking.** A recorded weekly monologue from week 1; from week 15, a timed 3-4 minute talk on the week's theme plus a role-play. A weekly conversation partner or tutor helps most here.
- **Use of Greek.** The app's Practice sets are this paper's drill. In the exam phase, redo every B1-B2 lesson's Practice once.
