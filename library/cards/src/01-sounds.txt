# alphabet | A1 | Το αλφάβητο | The alphabet

| Letter | Name | Sound | Letter | Name | Sound |
|-|-|-|-|-|-|
| Α α | άλφα | a (father) | Ν ν | νι | n |
| Β β | βήτα | v | Ξ ξ | ξι | ks |
| Γ γ | γάμμα | gh; y before ε, ι sounds | Ο ο | όμικρον | o |
| Δ δ | δέλτα | th (this) | Π π | πι | p |
| Ε ε | έψιλον | e (pet) | Ρ ρ | ρο | r (tapped) |
| Ζ ζ | ζήτα | z | Σ σ ς | σίγμα | s (z before β, γ, δ, μ ...) |
| Η η | ήτα | i (machine) | Τ τ | ταυ | t |
| Θ θ | θήτα | th (thin) | Υ υ | ύψιλον | i |
| Ι ι | γιώτα | i; y before a vowel | Φ φ | φι | f |
| Κ κ | κάππα | k | Χ χ | χι | kh (loch); ç before ε, ι sounds |
| Λ λ | λάμδα | l | Ψ ψ | ψι | ps |
| Μ μ | μι | m | Ω ω | ωμέγα | o |

- Five letters or pairs spell the sound **i**: **η, ι, υ, ει, οι** (and **υι**). Two spell **o**: **ο, ω**. Two spell **e**: **ε, αι**.
- **ς** is used only at the end of a word: **σεισμός**.
- Every letter is pronounced; there are no silent letters except in borrowed words.

! **Γ** before **ε, αι, ι, η, υ, ει, οι** sounds like English "y": **γεια, γυναίκα**. **Χ** in the same place sounds like German "ich": **χέρι, όχι**.

| γγ | ng | Αγγλία |
| γχ | nkh | ελέγχω |
| τσ | ts | τσάι |
| τζ | dz | τζάμι |

- A **diaeresis** (¨) splits a pair into two vowels: **κοροϊδεύω** (ο-ι), **Αϊ-Βασίλης**, **καΐκι**, **Μαΐου**. When the accent is on the first vowel, the pair is already split and needs no diaeresis: **τσάι, πλάι, ρολόι**.
- **ι** or **υ** before a vowel and after a consonant often glides to "y": **ποιος** (pyos), **ίσιος** (ísyos), **βιβλία** (vivlía, full i).

# stress | A1 | Ο τόνος | Stress and the accent mark

- Every word of two or more syllables has **one** accent (τόνος) on the stressed vowel: **καφές, θάλασσα, μήλο**.
- One-syllable words have no accent: **και, το, μου, τι** ... except the pairs that need one to be told apart: **ή** "or" (η "the"), **πού** "where?" (που "that"), **πώς** "how?" (πως "that").
- Stress can fall only on one of the **last three** syllables.
- On a pair, the accent goes on the **second** vowel: **ούτε, είναι, παίζω**. On capitals at the start of a word it stands before the letter: **Όλγα, Άρης**. All-capital words take no accent: **ΑΘΗΝΑ**.

| Changing stress changes the word | |
|-|-|
| **πότε** "when?" | **ποτέ** "never" |
| **γέρος** "old man" | **γερός** "strong" |
| **νόμος** "law" | **νομός** "prefecture" |
| **χώρος** "space" | **χορός** "dance" |

**The extra accent.** When a weak pronoun follows a word stressed on its third-from-last syllable, the word gets a second accent on its last syllable: **το αυτοκίνητό μου, διάβασέ το, βλέποντάς τον**. With two pronouns after a word stressed on its second-from-last syllable: **δώσε μού το**.

! **Ο φίλος μου είπε** "my friend said" but **Ο φίλος μού είπε** "the friend told me": here an accent on the weak pronoun shows it belongs to the verb.
