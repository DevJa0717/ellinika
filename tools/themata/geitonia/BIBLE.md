# «Μια χρονιά στην Αιγίνης 14» — series bible (fiction, B1–B2)

Setting: a 1970s six-storey πολυκατοικία at οδός Αιγίνης 14, Κυψέλη, Athens. Plateia Kypselis and Fokionos Negri pedestrian street nearby. Story year: September to August. Light, warm, humorous tone with some drama; no graphic violence or explicit sex (slang/vulgar target words appear in characters' dialogue or are discussed, e.g. the grandmother scolding, a teenager's lyrics, a football terrace).

Cast (keep names/facts consistent):
- Ground floor: κυρ-Αντώνης Βλάχος (72), retired Navy petty officer (συνταξιούχος κελευστής), from Volos, the block's διαχειριστής; stubborn, tells navy stories, watches the news loudly. Κυρία Ρούλα Βλάχου (68), his wife, great cook, churchgoer, gossip, soft heart.
- Corner shop (ground floor): Τζαβέντ Αχμέντ (31), born in Lahore, in Greece since age 12, runs the mini market «Η Γωνιά», fluent Greek, cricket and football fan, dry humour.
- 2nd floor: Στέλιος Καραγιάννης (34), αρχιφύλακας at the Kypseli police station, honest, tired, good cook. His girlfriend Μαρίνα Κωστοπούλου (29), nurse at Ευαγγελισμός hospital, night shifts, from Crete (Chania).
- 3rd floor: Ελένη Μαυρίδη (45), crime/court reporter for a news site; Νίκος Μαυρίδης (48), PE teacher and coach of an amateur basketball team; their daughter Δανάη (16), writes rap lyrics, surfs in summer, sharp tongue.
- 4th floor: Βέρα Ζαφειρίου (38), economist and business journalist (freelance), single mother of Λευτέρης (8), who loves dinosaurs, cards and magic tricks.
- 5th floor: Άρης Λαμπρόπουλος (24), striker (σέντερ φορ) at a Super League 2 club, from Patras; flatmate Γιώργος Πετρίδης (26), trainee lawyer (ασκούμενος δικηγόρος) at a criminal law firm.
- Penthouse (ρετιρέ): empty at first; in winter Mr Φραγκίσκος Ρενιέρης (55), mysterious new owner, moves in (turns out to be a retired magician/escape artist). Optional.

Parts (each is written by a different author; keep to your months, but other characters may appear):
- part1 Σεπτέμβριος–Οκτώβριος: back to school, the football season, Άρης's goals, Νίκος's team, tennis.
- part2 Νοέμβριος–Δεκέμβριος: a burglary in the block, Στέλιος and Γιώργος, a court case Ελένη covers.
- part3 Ιανουάριος–Φεβρουάριος: news, politics, money: Βέρα's articles, Αντώνης arguing with the TV, the block's budget, Carnival.
- part4 Μάρτιος–Απρίλιος: Μαρίνα's hospital, illness and spring; Easter in Αντώνης's village on Pelion near Volos, nature.
- part5 Μάιος–Ιούνιος: Δανάη's rap, exams, teen slang, Ρούλα's kitchen, a party in the block.
- part6 Ιούλιος–Αύγουστος: summer, islands, the heat, holidays, the block empties, a finale.
