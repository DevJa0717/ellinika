# Sources: slang-crete (Κρητικά)

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cretan_Greek: southern velar affrication ([tɕe] for "and"); ίντα/γιάντα; μπλιο = πια; πράμα = nothing/anything; θωρώ, θέτω; αίγα. (The article's English glosses have errors, e.g. it gives "thinks" for θωρεί; corrected from the other sources below.)
- https://el.wikipedia.org/wiki/Κρητική_διάλεκτος: clitics after the verb (κατέχω το, γροικώ ντου); τως/ντως; τση/τσι; -ξα aorists (εζύγωξα, εστέγνωξα); ε-/ή- augment (ή- is eastern); hiatus avoidance (τρώγω, λέγω; βαρά, μερά, γρά); final -ν only before vowels; Pashley's note on λ→ρ; vocabulary list (θωρώ, γρικώ, κατέχω, ξανοίγω, μανίζω, μολάρω, (α)γλακώ, επά/επαέ, ετά, εδά, ντελόγο, ταχιά, οψάργας, σιμά, αλάργο, όντε, απής, μαντινιάδα).
- https://www.greek-language.gr/greekLang/modern_greek/studies/dialects/thema_b_6/index.html (Πύλη για την ελληνική γλώσσα): τ/δ + glide → θ/δ (μάθια); gen. pl. without -ν; no nasal before b/d/g; cluster simplification (άθρωπος); α-/ο- prefixes (οφέτος); -ομε 1pl; -εύγω verbs; να + verb + θέλω future; -ξ- aorists; clitic after verb; κοπέλλι, κουζουλός, κουράδι (flock), εδά, επαέ; πράμα = nothing, θέτω = sleep, χτήμα = donkey, πυροβόλος = lighter.
- Manolessou & Pantelidis, "Velar fronting in Modern Greek dialects", MGDLT (https://pasithee.library.upatras.gr/mgdlt/article/download/2599/2836): the three fronting zones of Crete (mountain Chania standard-like; rest of Chania + Rethymno strongly fronted [c]; east Rethymno, Heraklion, Lasithi alveolo-palatal [tɕ dʑ ɕ ʑ]); Kontosopoulos's observation that Cretans in Athens soften the eastern type; χωριό → [xorˈʑo]; Cyprus has palato-alveolar [tʃ ʃ].
- https://www.e-thrapsano.gr/cretan-dialect (Thrapsano, Heraklion glossary), letters α, γ, ε, θ, κ, μ, ο, σ, τ: ανε = if; αθιβολή = conversation; αλάργο; γιάντα; γιάε = look; γροικώ; εδά; είντα; εκειά; θέτω = lie down; θωρώ; κατέχω = know; κοπέλι = small child; κουζουλός; κουράδι; μανίζω; μολάρω; οφέτος; οψάργας; οψές; ογλήγορα; σύντεκνος = baptismal koumbaros; σπολάτι = εις πολλά έτη.
- https://www.e-thrapsano.gr/cretan-dialect-articles?start=4: κουζουλός, πράμα = nothing, πυροβόλος = lighter, σπολλάτη, ντελικανής (Turkish loan).
- Ariadne (University of Crete journal), https://ejournals.lib.uoc.gr/Ariadne/article/download/1114/1014/2023, seen through a search summary: ζάβαλε is an assertive/interjectional particle in current use, against Pangalos's dictionary gloss "unfortunately" (Turkish zavallı).
- https://www.greek-language.gr/greekLang/modern_greek/studies/dialects/thema_b_6/links.html: list of Cretan dictionaries and glossaries (Xanthinakis, Pangalos and others), used to find sources.
- https://research.utu.fi/converis/portal/detail/Publication/499997688 (Psaltaki, on ίντα and γιάντα): confirms both as the Cretan interrogatives (title and abstract only).
- From general knowledge, not checked against a dedicated source: φιλότιμο, μπέσα, λεβεντιά, βεντέτα, μπαλωθιές, ριζίτικα, τσικουδιά/ρακή, the mantinada metre (8+7 fifteen-syllable line), Heraklion = "Κάστρο". The mantinades in the lesson are original compositions.
- Partly verified: ετσά (heard and sung, but no dictionary entry found); ανάλεμα as a curse; λ→ρ (only as a historical note). Not used (could not verify): κερασάκια, αποσπερίζω as specifically Cretan.
