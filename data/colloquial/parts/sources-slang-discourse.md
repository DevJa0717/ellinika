# Sources: slang-discourse

- https://el.wiktionary.org/wiki/μωρέ — etymology of μωρέ (vocative of μωρός), its uses with names and for surprise, irony or sympathy; basis of the culture note on μωρέ > ωρέ > ρε.
- https://el.wiktionary.org/wiki/ρε — ρε < ωρέ < μωρέ; colloquial senses 'μωρέ/μωρή' and 'hey, friend'.
- https://el.wiktionary.org/wiki/μπα — three uses of μπα: surprise (often ironic), uncertain denial, strong disapproval.
- https://el.wiktionary.org/wiki/όπα — όπα as an interjection of admiration, irony or surprise, and as a dance cheer.
- https://el.wiktionary.org/wiki/ε — ε as a marker of puzzlement or surprise and as a vocative call ('ε, συ, έλα εδώ').
- https://el.wiktionary.org/wiki/τέλος_πάντων — the two functions: topic change and grudging acceptance.
- https://el.wiktionary.org/wiki/καλά — standard senses of καλά (used as a baseline for the colloquial/intonational uses).
- https://el.wiktionary.org/wiki/καλά_κρασιά — the ironic modern use (reply to nonsense; expression of disappointment).
- https://www.flowmagazine.gr/kala_krasia/ — καλά κρασιά is said ironically and is mocking rather than a real wish.
- https://www.thewinelovers.gr/post/πώς-ξεκίνησε-η-φράση-καλά-κρασιά — origin as a grape-harvest wish (friends touring tavernas for the new wine) and the later ironic shift.
- http://www.psarikorinthias.gr/newsDetailed.asp?id=1096 — evidence that the sincere harvest wish ('καλά κρασιά, καλόπιοτα') still exists in villages.
- Core lexicon /mnt/project-files/data/lexicon/core-1000.json and core-3000.json — checked which particles (βρε, άντε, μπα, όπα, δηλαδή, λοιπόν, εντάξει, αμάν, τέλος πάντων) already had entries, to avoid duplicating them.
- Existing lessons b2-particles.json and b2-discourse.json in app/lessons — checked their scope so this unit did not repeat them.
