# Sources: slang-youth (Αργκό των νέων και του ίντερνετ)

Consulted 2026-10-18. slang.gr pages returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch; their content was read through search-engine extracts of the entry pages listed.

- https://www.alfavita.gr/ekpaideysi/498885_krintzaro-flexaro-mpifaro-i-glossa-tis-gen-z-poy-xanagrafei-ta-ellinika : Gen Z glossary (cringe/κριντζάρω, diss/ντισάρω, expose, flex/φλεξάρω, κριπουλιάζω, φασαίος, γκαφρά < φράγκα, τέπο < ποτέ, μπρικάρω, μπροκολόκο, μπιντζάρω, βασικός, μπιφάρω, Greeklish among teens); comment by linguist Γ. Ξυδόπουλος that this creativity is part of teen socialisation.
- https://www.alfavita.gr/koinonia/463418_mama-min-krintzareis-kai-alles-14-lexeis-gia-na-epikoinoneite-kalytera-me-paidi-sas : same glossary in a 'for parents' framing; title «Μαμά, μην κριντζάρεις» used for the parent-teen angle.
- https://www.alfavita.gr/koinonia/475396_milas-ti-neaniki-odigos-epibiosis-sti-glossa-ton-efibon : bro, delulu, cringe, flex, diss, «έχω beef», scroll, and the texting abbreviation list (ακλ, ν, ξρ, πρμν, τπτ, γτ, δν, γνκ, εχμ, ββ, δλδ, κλ, κκ = οκ, τέσπα); comment by Φ. Παναγιωτίδης.
- https://www.cnn.gr/ellada/story/518058/i-allagi-den-einai-apeili-to-glossari-tis-neolaias-kai-pos-metamorfonei-ta-ellinika : «δίνω content», bro for either sex, «τι φάση», -άρω verbs (σκρολάρω, γκουγκλάρω, γκριντζάρω, φλεξάρω), κουλ/φαν/ροκάς as older loans; linguist Ε. Μαγουλά on why slang is not a threat.
- https://www.alfavita.gr/koinonia/474839_eiste-nteloyloy-xerete-ti-simainei (and 511928, 486426 variants) : ντελούλου, TikTok origin, meaning.
- https://www.alfavita.gr/ekpaideysi/487757_pos-i-genia-z-leei-s-agapo : 143 / 1543 / 1437 number codes and emoji codes.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greeklish : phonetic vs orthographic Greeklish, digit substitutions (8 = θ, 3 = ξ, 4 = ψ), decline after Unicode, forum bans, business use seen as unprofessional.
- https://www.slang.gr/lemma/3358-tpt : tpt/τπτ = τίποτα in chat.
- https://www.slang.gr/definition/26378-tsilare and https://www.slang.gr/lemma/66-tsil : τσίλαρε / τσιλάρω / τσιλ.
- https://www.slang.gr/lemma/2646-tzertzelos and https://www.slang.gr/lemma/7259-tzertzeles : τζέρτζελος/τζερτζελές, etymology from Turkish zelzele.
- https://www.slang.gr/definition/11747-kapsouris : καψούρης, κάψα, καψούρα (often unrequited).
- https://www.slang.gr/lemma/3723-floros , https://www.slang.gr/lemma/878-kafros , https://www.slang.gr/lemma/6064-ksenerotos : φλώρος (wimp, mummy's boy, effeminacy undertone), κάφρος (boor; racist origin via Arabic 'unbeliever'), ξενέρωτος (boring; etymology paradox; Thessaloniki nuance).
- https://www.slang.gr/lemma/48-kagkouras : κάγκουρας, regional senses (Thessaloniki, Volos), καγκούρισσα.
- https://www.slang.gr/lemma/2426-akyro and https://www.slang.gr/lemma/484-akyros : άκυρο interjection, άκυρος person, «έφαγα άκυρο».
- https://www.slang.gr/lemma/5871-mpro and https://www.slang.gr/lemma/11872-ksa : μπρο (rap circles), ξα (cousin).
- https://www.slang.gr/lemma/112-rispek , https://www.slang.gr/definition/6220-tespa , https://www.slang.gr/lemma/1231-lol : ρισπέκ(τ), τέσπα, λολ / λολάρω (often ironic, overused).
- https://www.slang.gr/lemma/26233-gkostingk : γκόστινγκ (ghosting). No slang.gr entry was found for γκοστάρω, so it is marked as less settled.
- slang.gr entry for βαϊμπάρω (via search extract of slang.gr definitions listing) : βαϊμπάρω, example with sunset.
- https://www.skai.gr/news/technology/viber-to-2021-oi-ellines-kseperasan-tis-90-ekat-ores-se-synomilies : Viber on 90%+ of Greek smartphones (company figures, 2021).
- https://exams.ie/paper-files/lc/modern-greek/higher/pp/2018/p0/paper.pdf : teenagers dropping vowels in written chat (consonant-skeleton abbreviations).

Not verified (flagged in lesson, not taught for production): σιπάρω (ship), λιτ (lit), φιξ, σλ, τλκ. νμζ = νομίζω follows the documented pattern but was not found in a source list.
