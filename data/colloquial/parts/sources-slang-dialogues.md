# Sources: slang-dialogues (Σκηνές από την καθημερινότητα)

Consulted 2026-10-18. Scenes and dialogues are original; the sources below were used to check meanings, register and cultural details.

- https://www.slang.gr/lemma/5871-mpro and https://www.slang.gr/lemma/18270-aderfe : forms of address (μπρο, αδερφέ) used to calibrate ρε μεγάλε / φίλε μου among strangers.
- https://www.slang.gr/lemma/2646-tzertzelos , https://www.slang.gr/lemma/7259-tzertzeles : register of noisy fun / commotion vocabulary.
- https://www.slang.gr/lemma/2426-akyro : «άκυρο» as a refusal used in the register-switching table (friend column).
- https://www.slang.gr/lemma/26233-gkostingk : γκόστινγκ, used in the breakup scene.
- https://www.slang.gr/lemma/22266-aima-kai-ammos and https://www.slang.gr/lemma/1230-artsi-mpourtsi-kai-loulas : πανικός / χαμός-type chaos vocabulary (football, disorder).
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laiki_agora : λαϊκή αγορά as a weekly neighbourhood farmers' market and social custom (culture block, scene 7).
- https://www.skai.gr/news/technology/viber-to-2021-oi-ellines-kseperasan-tis-90-ekat-ores-se-synomilies : Viber as default channel (boss-on-Viber column of the register table).
- https://academia.library.upatras.gr/icgl/article/download/3653/3698 : paper on lemmatising Greek football vocabulary; confirmed there is no corpus-based fan-slang dictionary, so football items were restricted to widely known terms (γκολάρα, πουλημένος διαιτητής, δοκάρι, ρεβάνς, οφσάιντ, πέναλτι).
- https://www.cnn.gr/ellada/story/518058/i-allagi-den-einai-apeili-to-glossari-tis-neolaias-kai-pos-metamorfonei-ta-ellinika : -άρω verbs and 'τι φάση' (background for the café scene register).
- Cross-check against this project's own lesson app/lessons/theme-work.json (everyday vs formal work vocabulary: τα παράτησα / παραιτήθηκα, με διώξανε / απολύθηκα) for the colloquial ↔ formal table.

Searches for documented market-trader calls («πάρ' τα, πάρ' τα», «όλα ένα ευρώ») and fan-chant lexicons returned nothing citable; those lines rest on general knowledge of Greek usage and are standard, non-controversial phrases.
